Selected work

A small practice. Most of the work is houses and the slow parts of public buildings: the room you wait in, the court you cross, the stair you remember. Drawings are the point. Photographs come later, and only if they tell the truth about the light.

01

Courtyard House

Frederiksberg · 2025

A family house organized around a brick court. The rooms borrow light from the middle and give the street almost nothing.

 02

Reading Room

Aarhus · 2024

An addition to a branch library. One long table, a high window, and shelves that stop before the ceiling so the room stays a room.

 03

Kiln Pavilion

Bornholm · 2023

A seasonal workspace for a ceramics studio. Timber, a thick roof, and a door wide enough for a kiln.

 04

Landing

Malmö · 2022

The arrival hall of a small clinic. Benches in the light, desks out of it, and a material palette a person can touch without thinking.

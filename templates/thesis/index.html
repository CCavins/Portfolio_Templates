About Publications Teaching Talks

I study how language models handle languages with very little text: how they fail, what makes a small amount of good data worth more than a large amount of scraped data, and how speakers of those languages can evaluate a system without trusting the people who built it.

Before Edinburgh I did a postdoc at Saarland and a PhD at Cambridge. I grew up between Beirut and Montréal, which is probably why I care about this.

Services

  1. Identity

    Name, mark, type, and the rules a factory floor can follow. Built to survive being printed on a pallet.

  2. Product design

    Interfaces for tools people use all day: dashboards, configurators, field apps. Research first, then screens.

  3. Websites

    Marketing sites and catalogues that your own team can update. We hand over the keys and a manual.

How we work

  1. We visit

    Every project starts on your premises. You cannot design a configurator for a machine you have not watched someone configure.

  2. Small team, whole way

    The people in the pitch are the people on the project. Two or three of us, start to finish, with no handoff to juniors.

  3. You can run it after

    Files, fonts, templates, and a training session. We would rather be rehired for the next thing than needed for this one.

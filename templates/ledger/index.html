How an engagement runs

  1. A 30-minute call

    You describe the company and the decision you are stuck on. I say whether I can help and what it would cost.

  2. Two weeks of listening

    I read everything: bank, ledger, contracts, the old deck. You get a memo of what the numbers actually say.

  3. The work

    A model, a price list, or a reporting rhythm, built with whoever on your team will own it after me.

  4. A handover

    Documentation, a recorded walkthrough, and a standing offer to answer questions for the next quarter.

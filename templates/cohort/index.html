Questions people ask

Do you run workshops for non-native English speakers?

Yes, and about half of every cohort is. The methods are about structure and the reader, not grammar, and they transfer across languages.

Can you tailor a session to our documents?

That is the default. Send three real examples a week ahead; I anonymise them and build the session around what I find.

What does coaching cost?

Six sessions over eight weeks is £1,400. A small number of reduced-fee places are kept for students and people between jobs; just ask.

Do you travel?

Within the UK and Europe, yes. Elsewhere, remote works well and I have done it a lot.

04 — Notes

On naming food

Northwind’s first list sounded like a startup. The name that stayed was already painted on the original awning. We just let it be the whole brand.

On clinical language

Lumen’s product words had leaked into the waiting room. The fix was a short lexicon: words for patients, words for software, and a rule about which door they use.

On saying no in print

Paperplane’s editors knew what they loved. The brand work was writing down what they would decline, so the next pitch could be judged in a sentence.
